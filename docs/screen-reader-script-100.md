# Screen-reader walk for Booklet 1.0.0: your script

For Ben, on the Mac (VoiceOver in Obsidian) and on the iPhone (VoiceOver in the Obsidian app). About 25 minutes for each device. Nothing here is about a real person: the note is made up.

## Before you start

1. Put the folder **Screen reader walk** (from the plugin's `test/page-notes` folder) into your vault. It holds one note, `Screen reader walk.booklet.md`, and a `media` folder with a three-second tone. On the iPhone, let your vault sync so the folder arrives.
2. Turn VoiceOver on. Mac: press Command + F5. iPhone: say "Hey Siri, turn on VoiceOver", or Settings, Accessibility, VoiceOver.
3. Open the note. Mac: Command + O, type `Screen reader walk`, press Return. iPhone: find it in the file list and tap it twice (VoiceOver's "tap").
4. How to move. Mac: Control + Option + Right arrow goes to the next thing (the plugin's page is a normal web page, so Tab also works). iPhone: swipe right for next, left for back, double-tap to press.

Under each step, write what really happened. "Heard" can be rough. A step that went wrong is the useful result, so write it down even if it is only "it said nothing".

## The walk

**1. The card that opens by itself.** The note names a made-up site that is not allowed yet, so a card opens when the note does.
- Do this: open the note (step 3 above). Listen.
- You should hear: a dialog, titled something like "This booklet plays media from other sites". You should be able to move only among the card's own buttons ("Allow for this note", "Allow for this vault", "Not now"), each with the site's name.
- What happened: ______________________________

**2. Close the card.**
- Do this: press Escape (Mac), or find "Not now" and press it (iPhone).
- You should hear: the card goes, and you land back on the note, not on nothing.
- What happened: ______________________________

**3. The tab bar.**
- Do this: move to the three tabs at the top of the page.
- You should hear: a group named "Screen reader walk", then "Read, tab, selected", then "Answer", then "Listen". On the Mac, the Left and Right arrow keys should move between tabs. On the iPhone, double-tap a tab to choose it.
- What happened: ______________________________

**4. Move through a page.**
- Do this: go to the Read tab and move forward one thing at a time.
- You should hear: the page title ("The first page", as a heading), some words with two links in them, then a "Back" or "Next: The second page" button.
- What happened: ______________________________

**5. The Pages button.**
- Do this: find the button "Pages" and press it. Move through what opens. Mac: press Escape. iPhone: press "Close".
- You should hear: "Pages, button, collapsed" before, "expanded" after. Then a search box, a Close button, and a list of links; the page you are on should be said to be the current one. Choosing a link should say the new page's title. Closing should put you back on the "Pages" button.
- What happened: ______________________________

**6. A choice and a multiple choice.** Go to the Answer tab.
- Do this: choose "Pear", then "Pencil" and "Ruler". On the multiple choice, type a word of your own (for example `Glue`) in its field and press Return.
- You should hear: each button with whether it is chosen or not, changing when you press it. The question is said as a group before its buttons.
- What happened: ______________________________

**7. A scale.**
- Do this: choose "2 Fairly sure".
- You should hear: the number and the words together, and "chosen" (or "on") afterwards.
- What happened: ______________________________

**8. Words, a number and a date.** Press "Next: Writing".
- Do this: type a few words in the long box; type a number in the number box; set a date.
- You should hear: each box named by its question, not "edit text" alone. The date box should say which part you are in (month, day, year).
- What happened: ______________________________

**9. The Allow list.**
- Do this: press the "Allow list" button at the top of the page. Move through the card. Press Close, or Escape.
- You should hear: a dialog called "Allow list", two headed lists with a tick box for each site, a box called "Add a site", and Close. Closing returns you to the "Allow list" button.
- What happened: ______________________________

**10. Play and pause the tone.** Go to the Listen tab.
- Do this: find the player for "A short tone". Press play. Press pause.
- You should hear: a play button or an audio player named after the tone; the tone sounds; pausing stops it. The "Done" box under it can be left alone.
- What happened: ______________________________

**11. The Now playing bar.**
- Do this: play the tone again, then go to the Read tab while it plays.
- You should hear: a bar at the bottom with the tone's name, and the buttons "Back to the page" and "Close". "Close" should stop the sound.
- What happened: ______________________________

**12. Anything else.**
- Anything that was confusing, said twice, said nothing, or lost your place: ______________________________

When you are done, turn VoiceOver off (Command + F5 on the Mac; "Hey Siri, turn off VoiceOver" on the iPhone). Android was not tried.
