# Booklet in Obsidian: a short guide

Booklet is an add-on (Obsidian calls it a plugin) that opens a Booklet note as a page you can tap and fill in. Your answers are saved inside the note itself, on your own device. Nothing about you is sent anywhere by Booklet. The one time your device contacts another website is when you choose to allow a site, such as YouTube, and then press Play on its video or audio.

## 1. Install it

Booklet is not in Obsidian's list of add-ons yet. Until it is, you will not find it in the steps below. These steps are for **when the plugin is listed**.

1. Open **Settings** (the gear at the bottom left).
2. Choose **Community plugins**. If Obsidian says "Restricted mode" is on, press the button to turn it off.
3. Press **Browse**.
4. Type **Booklet** in the search box and choose it.
5. Press **Install**, then **Enable**.

## 2. If you use Obsidian Sync

Obsidian does not copy add-ons to your other devices unless you ask it to. Do this once, on each device:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Sync**.
2. Find **Vault configuration sync**.
3. Turn on **Active community plugin list** and **Installed community plugin list**.
4. Restart Obsidian if it asks.

On your other devices, also make sure Community plugins is turned on (step 2 above). If you do not use Sync, skip this part.

## 3. Keep it up to date

Add-ons do not update by themselves.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Community plugins**.
2. Press **Check for updates**.
3. If Booklet is listed, press **Update**.

## Good to know

- Booklet is tested on a computer. An early version worked on an iPhone. It has not been tried on Android.
- If something looks wrong, tell whoever gave you your Booklet notes. Do not delete the note: your answers are in it.
