# Booklet in Obsidian: a short guide

Booklet is an add-on (Obsidian calls it a plugin) that opens a Booklet note as a page you can tap and fill in. Your answers are saved inside the note itself, on your own device. Nothing about you is sent anywhere by Booklet. The one time your device contacts another website is when you choose to allow a site, such as YouTube, and then press Play on its video or audio.

## 1. Install it

Booklet is in Obsidian's list of add-ons. It needs Obsidian 1.11.7 or later. The same steps, with pictures for a computer and a phone, are at https://bookletmd.org/obsidian/.

1. Open **Settings**: the gear at the bottom left, beside your vault's name. On a phone, tap the sidebar button at the top left first, then the gear.
2. Choose **Community plugins**. If Restricted mode is on, press **Exit Restricted mode**, beside or under **Allow community plugins**.
3. Press **Browse**.
4. Type **Booklet** in the search box and choose **Booklet**, by Benjamin Armstrong III.
5. Press **Install**. When it has installed, press **Enable**.

## 2. If you use Obsidian Sync

Obsidian does not copy add-ons to your other devices unless you ask it to. Do this once, on each device:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Sync**.
2. Find **Vault configuration sync**.
3. Turn on **Active community plugin list** and **Installed community plugin list**.
4. Restart Obsidian if it asks.

On your other devices, also make sure Community plugins is turned on (step 2 above). If you do not use Sync, skip this part.

## 3. Keep it up to date

Add-ons do not update by themselves.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Community plugins**.
2. Press **Check for updates**.
3. If Booklet is listed, press **Update**.

## Good to know

- If you write booklets, the command **Booklet: Editor mode** (or the pencil button on a booklet's view) opens a *Booklet blocks* panel beside the note: add a piece at the cursor, change the settings of the line the cursor is on, and see an outline and the problems. Before it changes something that your readers' saved answers or rows depend on, it says how many it touches and asks. The panel is in English only.
- Booklet is tested on a computer, and each release is tried on an iPhone. It has not been tried on Android.
- If something looks wrong, tell whoever gave you your Booklet notes. Do not delete the note: your answers are in it.
